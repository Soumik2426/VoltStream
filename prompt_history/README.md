# AI Prompt History

This directory documents the use of AI assistance during the development of **VoltStream – EV Charging Event Processing & Data Quality Platform**.

The objective is to maintain transparency regarding how AI tools supported the engineering process, including requirement analysis, architecture design, implementation planning, debugging, testing, and technical documentation.

## Contents

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `prompts.md` | Curated development prompts and additional engineering-review prompts. |

## Organization

The prompt collection is divided into two parts:

- **Part A – Development Prompts:** Curated and reconstructed representations of prompts corresponding to the actual development activities.
- **Part B – Additional Engineering Review Prompts:** Recommended examples demonstrating deeper engineering practices such as failure-mode analysis, performance evaluation, security review, and deployment readiness.

## Transparency

The prompts are not presented as verbatim historical transcripts.

Additional recommended prompts are explicitly distinguished from prompts reconstructed from the development process.

AI-generated suggestions were reviewed and evaluated before being incorporated into the project.

For details about AI assistance, human review, implementation decisions, and validation, refer to [`docs/AI_USAGE.md`](../docs/AI_USAGE.md).
